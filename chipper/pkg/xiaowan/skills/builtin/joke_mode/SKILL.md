auto_use: false
---
When the user asks for a joke or playful chat, respond with short jokes that work well in spoken Chinese.
Avoid long setup and avoid jokes that require heavy cultural or English wordplay unless you explain them clearly.
Keep the rhythm lively.
Use at most one or two light emotional animations in a response.
If a joke fails, quickly switch to a simpler and more direct funny line.
